Frequently asked questions

How do I play or change a sound?

Tap the moon on the home screen to play, and tap again to pause. Swipe sideways to switch sounds, swipe up for the sleep timer, and swipe down to open your library.

Does the sound stop when the screen turns off?

No. Sound keeps playing with the screen off or while you use other apps. To stop it, tap the moon again or use the playback controls on the Lock Screen.

What happens when the sleep timer ends?

The sound doesn't cut out. It slowly fades away and then stops, so the ending won't wake you up.

My alarm didn't go off.

Dalbit needs permission to set alarms, which it asks for the first time you create one. Check that alarms are allowed in Settings › Dalbit.

Where are my mixes saved?

Your mixes, alarms and settings are stored only on this device. Deleting the app deletes them too.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions, choose Dalbit and cancel. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

I switched phones and lost Premium.

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases on Dalbit's subscription screen.
